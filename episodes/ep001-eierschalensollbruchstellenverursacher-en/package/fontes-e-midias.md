# Fontes e registro de mídias — Impact egg topper ("Eierschalensollbruchstellenverursacher")

Pesquisa de 2026-10-10. Método: Agente de pesquisa (WebSearch/WebFetch/Firecrawl, APIs Openverse e Wikimedia) + verificacao manual do candidato escolhido na pagina do fabricante, Wikipedia (de) e DWDS.

## Produto

- **O que é:** A metal bell placed over the top of a boiled egg; a ball is lifted on a rod and dropped, and the impact cracks the shell in a line around the top, which can then be removed.
- **Uso legítimo / público:** People who eat soft-boiled eggs from an egg cup, a common German breakfast habit: it avoids shell splinters and cutting the top with a knife. No source consulted describes it as assistive technology.
- **Risco de a piada atingir quem não deve:** Low. The joke is the salesman's exaggeration and the mismatch between device and task; do not mock the manufacturer, the designers or people who like the device.
- **Preço consultado:** EUR 28,95 em 2026-10-10 (fonte s1). Color Edition in the manufacturer's store; 19% VAT included, shipping extra. Other editions: 29.95 to 31.95 EUR.

## Fatos verificados

- **f1** — The German manufacturer Take2-Design calls its "Clack" egg topper an Eierschalensollbruchstellenverursacher. _(fontes: s1)_
- **f2** — The word is in the DWDS dictionary, marked as usually jocular, for devices that crack a boiled egg's shell at a predetermined point. _(fontes: s3, s2)_
- **f3** — The word is made of Eierschale (eggshell), Sollbruchstelle (predetermined breaking point) and Verursacher (causer). _(fontes: s3)_
- **f4** — The word has 38 letters and 12 syllables. _(fontes: s3)_
- **f5** — Use according to the manufacturer: place the metal cap on the boiled egg, pull the ball up and let it go. _(fontes: s1)_
- **f6** — In the impact model, a bell with an inner edge is placed over the egg and the blow of a ball-shaped weight cracks the shell around the top. _(fontes: s2)_
- **f7** — According to the manufacturer, the top of the egg can then be removed by gently tilting the device or with the help of additional cutlery. _(fontes: s1)_
- **f8** — According to DWDS, the purpose is to eat the egg with a spoon without peeling it or cutting the top off with a knife. _(fontes: s3)_
- **f9** — The manufacturer credits the Clack design to three designers. _(fontes: s1)_
- **f10** — In the manufacturer's store, the Color Edition costs 28.95 euros (German VAT included, shipping extra), on 2026-10-10. _(fontes: s1)_
- **f11** — The manufacturer's store sells a Gem Edition with a gemstone ball (amethyst, rose quartz, green aventurine, sodalite) for 31.95 euros. _(fontes: s1)_
- **f12** — The manufacturer's store lists a Christmas edition (XMAS Edition). _(fontes: s1)_
- **f13** — There is a 1907 patent by Ferdinand Fleischmann for an egg opener with teeth and a scissor mechanism. _(fontes: s2)_
